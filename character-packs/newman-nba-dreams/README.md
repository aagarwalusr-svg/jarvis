# Big dreams start small

A 16-second original vertical cartoon featuring fictional childhood versions of Julian and Jaden Newman. The dialogue and depicted events are invented.

Play `../newman-nba-dreams.mp4` in a browser or standard video player. The file contains H.264 video at 720 × 1280, 24 fps, AAC audio, and burned-in subtitles. Separate captions are provided in `captions.srt`.

Julian dribbles and passes to Jaden, who takes a shot while they discuss going pro. Original synthetic scratch voices were generated with eSpeak NG; these are not recordings or voice clones of the real people. The music and basketball effects were synthesized for this clip. Character animation was rendered from original SVG drawings using Canvas and Chromium, then encoded with FFmpeg.
